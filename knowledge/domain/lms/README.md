# LMS — S5 Assortment Planning training (functional knowledge)

The full training curriculum from the S5 Products Guide, ingested verbatim as
three courses. This is the copilot's deepest **functional** source — what the
retail process is and how the S5 app supports it. Use it to explain concepts,
onboard, and fill the "functional" half of `knowledge_gained`. For quick term
lookups use `../glossary.md`; for the one-page model use
`../assortment-planning-primer.md`; come here for depth.

| Course | File | Covers |
|---|---|---|
| 1 — Concepts & Process | `01-assortment-planning-process.md` | Why AP matters, five success factors, objectives, pre/in-season, planning vs forecasting, and the Level-3 process (Selection → Ranging → Quantification → Reconciliation → Approve & Publish → Revision) |
| 2 — Retail Domain Foundations | `02-retail-domain-foundations.md` | Roles (design-led vs merchant-led), selling channels & omni/multi, currency/international, hierarchies, floorsets, attributes, lifecycle & seasonality, flow status, pricing & markdowns, price elasticity, size optimization, purchase orders |
| 3 — S5 Application Training | `03-s5-application-training.md` | App access/permissions, navigation, views/filters/flow-status; then module by module — Hindsighting (Collection/Canvas/Summary/Grid/Flow Type/Top TY vs LY…), Clustering, Assortment Strategy (target setting, planning grid), Assortment Build, Assortment Analysis, Reconciliation & Publish, Exception Analysis, In-Season |

These files are large (verbatim). When answering, cite the course + section
(e.g. "LMS Course 3 → Flow Status Definitions") rather than pasting long spans.
